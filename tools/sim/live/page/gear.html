The gear and the scope
What it is Not the board: what its jacks are plugged into, as in the smallest set-up. It is made in this page from the pitch CV and the gate shown under Outputs, and the board cannot tell it is there.
Patch Pitch CV to the VCO's pitch. Gate to the envelope, the envelope to the VCA's CV. The VCO through the VCA to the audio output.
Gear button Powers the gear. A browser plays nothing until it has been clicked.
VCO An octave up per volt. With the tune knob at its middle 0 V plays C, 130.81 Hz. White noise has no pitch and takes no notice of the CV.
Wavetables A row of waves the position pot goes through, the VCO playing a mix of the two it is between. Each is in the table below.
Position Where in the wavetable the VCO is. No use on the other waves.
Wavetable picture Shown while a wavetable is chosen: one cycle of its waves and of some between each two, the first at the front, lightest, and the last at the back, darkest. The thick line is the wave the position pot is at; the thin ones are the chorus's voices. The table is drawn solid, so a part of any of them that a nearer peak stands in front of is hidden.
Envelope, To position How far the envelope carries the VCO along the wavetable from where the position pot is: on every note, up the table at the attack's rate and back at the release's. At the top it is the whole table; at 0 it is off. The chorus's voices go with it. Needs the gear on; no use on the other waves.
Scan in the picture With the gear on, a bright length goes along the thick line from left to right and round again. Its speed is the pitch, from the pitch CV and the tune knob: once a second at 130.81 Hz, twice as often for each octave above, 8 times at most. It is far slower than the wave itself. Its brightness is the envelope, so it is dark between notes. Each of the chorus's lines has a fainter one.
Chorus More voices in the VCO, each a little off pitch, up to 15 cents either way: two as the pot leaves 0, and more the faster the further up it goes, about 8 at half way and 36 at the top, each coming in between the ones there. On a wavetable each also has its own place in the table: all at the position pot's with the chorus at 0, which is one wave, and going out from it as the pot comes up, until at the top they reach both ends of the table, standing closer together near the position pot's wave and further apart further out. On the other waves they are only off pitch. No use on noise.
Envelope Rises while the gate is high and stays up, then falls once it is low. Each time is how long it takes to get about 95% of the way.
Volume The listener's, after the audio output: the scope does not show it.
Scope, VCO out The VCO's wave before the VCA, a few milliseconds of it, held still on a rising crossing of zero.
Scope, VCO frequency The pitch the VCO is at, over the same four seconds as the trace under Outputs: the pitch CV and the tune knob together. The band is a whole number of octaves high, named in hertz, so a semitone is the same rise anywhere in it.
Scope, envelope The VCA's CV, over the same four seconds as the trace under Outputs.
Scope, audio out The same few milliseconds after the VCA: the VCO's wave at the height the envelope gives it.
Scope, axes On the two waves: a line at zero and at each peak, and one down every 5 ms. On the frequency: a line at each octave, named in hertz. On the envelope: its 0 and its 1. Those two have a line down at each second back from the present.
Pots Drag one up or down, or click it and use the arrow keys. Shift held makes a drag go slowly, and a double click puts a pot of the gear back where it started. A dimmed pot has no use on the wave chosen.
Limits Paced by the browser, not the chip, so nothing here is a measurement. Full scale is the VCO's own peak, not a voltage.
WavetableFrom its first wave to its last
Classic Sine, triangle, saw, square, 25% pulse, 10% pulse.
Harmonics A saw let through one harmonic at a time, 1 to 16, like a filter opening.
Odd harmonics A square built up one odd harmonic at a time, 1 to 15: hollow, then buzzing.
Pulse width A pulse narrowing from a square, 50% to 4%.
Formant A saw with a peak in its harmonics that goes up them, from harmonic 2 to 16, as a vowel's or a resonant filter's does.
Wavefolder A sine driven harder and harder into a folder, so its tops turn over on themselves, once to 8 times as hard.
Hard sync A saw started again with every cycle of the VCO and tuned up from it, from the same pitch to 4.5 times it.
