The watchdog, the crash log and what the button does
Watchdog A timer in the chip that resets it unless the firmware kicks it, which it does once a tick. It times out after about 256 ms.
Stop the tick The firmware never hangs by itself, so this stops its tick from outside: the loop goes on round with nothing to do, which is place 5. No other place can be reached from here.
After the reset The firmware starts as at power-on: it reads the panel afresh, comes up running and plays from the first step. The debug image's start-up line reads Reset = 8, and the midi image sends Start again.
Crash log Eight records in the EEPROM, used in turn. One is written after each watchdog reset, about 18 ms into the start-up. It is read here from the EEPROM, not from the serial log, so the release and midi images show it too.
Record Its number: 1 for the first, and round from 255 to 0. The newest is the one not followed by the next number.
Cause The reset flags, as in the start-up line: 8 is the watchdog.
Place What the firmware was doing: 1 past start-up with no tick run yet, 2 applying outputs, 3 gathering inputs, 4 in the core, 5 between ticks.
Count How many times running the same crash happened, 1 to 255. A crash with the cause and place of the newest record raises its count; any other takes the next slot.
To clear it Untick "Keep the EEPROM" and power cycle, which erases the calibration too. Nothing else clears it.
