MIDI in (D0, 31250 baud)

What MIDI in does
Which image Only the midi image listens. The debug image's serial pins are its log, and the release image never switches them on.
Clock 24 to a quarter note. Every sixth is an edge, as an edge on the clock input is: the first one takes the pattern over from the internal clock, and a step then begins on each, until the pattern is stopped.
Tempo pot, on the MIDI clock The same ratio as on the clock input, ÷4 to ×4, with six clocks counted as one edge.
Clock input and MIDI clock together The first of the two to give an edge since the last stop is the one followed. The other is passed over until the next stop.
Start Starts the pattern from its first step, running or not, on the MIDI clock: the step begins on the clock that follows. With no clock it waits.
Stop Stops the pattern, as the run button does, and forgets the external clock.
Continue Taken as a Start: the firmware cannot take a pattern up where it stopped.
Notes, and every other byte Passed over.
Power cycle Leaves the MIDI clock as it is.
