Calibration mode

The sequence is stopped. The gate is held high and the pitch CV sits at one point of the calibration table, to be measured.

Step 0's switches Pick the point, 0 to 4: one per octave.
Tempo pot Trims its voltage, a millivolt at a time.
Step 15's lowest switch Off and on again stores it. The gate drops for a quarter of a second to show it did.
To leave Change any switch on steps 12 to 15, then power cycle. Keep the EEPROM to play from what was stored.

The chart
What it shows Every signal on one chart, in two sections, each under its heading. Board: the clock input, the clock output, the gate, the reset input and the pitch CV. Patched gear: its four signals, while it is on. Each band's name is over it.
Colours By what a signal is, as the key at the chart's top right says: one for what comes into the board, one for what goes out of it, and one for the gear's, which is outside the board. A band's name is in its colour, and the gate and clock lights above are in the outputs'.
Time All but the last two bands are the last four seconds, the present at the right. The last two are each one wave, a few milliseconds across.
Clock in, Reset in A mark for each edge arriving at that input. Only the instant is known, not how long it was high.
VCO frequency The pitch the gear's VCO is at: the pitch CV and its tune knob together. The band is a whole number of octaves high, named in hertz, so a semitone is the same rise anywhere in it.
Envelope The gear's own, into its VCA's CV. The board's envelope CV is the figure above.
VCO out The VCO's wave before the VCA, held still on a rising crossing of zero.
Audio out The same few milliseconds after the VCA: the VCO's wave at the height the envelope gives it. The volume pot is after it and is not shown.
Axes A line across the pitch CV at each volt, across the frequency at each octave, at the envelope's 0 and 1, and at each wave's zero and peaks. A line down every four-second band at each second back from the present, and down each wave every 5 ms.
Limits The gear's bands are paced by the browser, not the chip, so nothing in them is a measurement. A wave's full scale is the VCO's own peak, not a voltage.
